How it works

How our photo booth rental works

No complicated setup, no confusing tech. Here is exactly what booking and event day look like.

  1. 01

    Hit us up

    Send an inquiry and we will get back to you with a package that suits your vibe. Your date is yours the moment the booking fee is paid and the contract is signed.

  2. 02

    Lock in the fun stuff

    Closer to the big day we send a quick form so you can choose your photo template, backdrop and any extra touches.

  3. 03

    We have got you

    On the day we handle everything. You just enjoy the party. We will make sure your guests have an epic time, and the best photos to prove it.

On the day

We handle everything

We arrive early to set up before your guests do. The booth needs a standard power outlet and Wi-Fi for instant sharing. Setup and breakdown time is not counted in your service hours.

  • Professional DSLR studio setup
  • Free delivery, setup and breakdown
  • A friendly on-site attendant
  • Instant sharing by QR, text and email
The details

Deposit, reschedules & insurance

Securing your date

A 50% deposit secures your date. Your booking is not reserved until the deposit is received and confirmed.

If plans change

Deposits are non-refundable but may be applied to a rescheduled date, subject to availability.

Fully insured

Enchanted Booth Co. carries general liability insurance and can provide a certificate of insurance (COI) on request.
