Good to know

Common questions

What types of events do you do photo booth rentals for?
Weddings, corporate events, holiday parties, birthdays, graduations and just about any celebration. See weddings, corporate events and parties.
What is included with every photo booth package?
Every booking includes a professional DSLR studio setup, free delivery, setup and breakdown, a friendly on-site attendant, your choice of backdrop, props, instant sharing by QR, text and email, and a full online gallery. See the packages for what each tier adds.
How much does it cost to rent a photo booth?
Packages start at $499 for 2 hours (Classic), $699 for 3 hours (Signature) and $899 for 4 hours (Premium). Additional hours are $100 each. See full packages and pricing, or read our photo booth cost guide for a full breakdown.
What kind of photo booth is it?
It is a modern open-air booth built around a professional DSLR camera, studio lighting and your choice of backdrop. Open-air means there is no cramped enclosed box, so big groups fit in one frame and every photo looks like a studio portrait. See the gallery and how it works.
Can you do company and holiday parties?
Absolutely. We run corporate events and holiday parties throughout the season with custom branded strips, a logo or message on the rear screen and a friendly attendant. December books out early, so reserve your date ahead of time.
What does setup and the experience look like?
We arrive early to set the booth up before your guests arrive. The booth needs a standard power outlet and Wi-Fi for instant sharing. A friendly attendant stays for the whole event, and we handle breakdown at the end. Setup and breakdown time is not counted in your service hours.
Do you travel? Are there travel fees?
Yes, we travel for events across the Des Moines metro and beyond. Travel within 30 miles of Waukee / West Des Moines is complimentary (40 miles on Premium). Beyond that: 30 to 60 miles is $100, 60 to 120 miles is $200, and 120+ miles is a custom quote. All fees are confirmed before you book. See service areas.
Are you insured?
Yes. Enchanted Booth Co. carries general liability insurance and can provide a certificate of insurance (COI) on request, which many venues require.
