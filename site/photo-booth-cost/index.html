Cost questions

Photo booth pricing FAQ

How much does a photo booth cost per hour in Des Moines?
Our packages are priced by the event rather than strictly by the hour, starting at $499 for two hours. If you want to keep the booth running longer, extra hours are a flat $100 each. Everything — the DSLR setup, studio lighting, unlimited prints and a friendly attendant — is already included in that price. See the full packages.
Why do some photo booths cost less?
Budget rentals are usually a tablet on a stand with no attendant and no prints. Every Enchanted Booth package is a professional DSLR booth with studio lighting, your choice of backdrop, unlimited custom photo strips and a real attendant from setup to breakdown — a different experience and a different photo.
Is a deposit required?
Yes. A 50% deposit secures your date and the balance is due before the event. Deposits are non-refundable but may be applied to a rescheduled date, subject to availability.
