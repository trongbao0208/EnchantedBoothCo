Privacy

Privacy & cookies

Enchanted Booth Co. (operated by Enchanted PhotoBooth LLC) respects your privacy. This page explains what we collect and why.

What we collect

When you submit a quote request, we collect the details you give us (name, email, phone, event date, location and any message) so we can respond to your inquiry and plan your event. We do not sell your information.

Event photos

Photos taken at your event belong to you and your guests. We may share selected photos in our gallery and on social media to showcase our work. If you would prefer we not feature your event, just let us know.

Cookies & analytics

This site uses minimal cookies for basic functionality and may use privacy-friendly analytics to understand how the site is used. No advertising trackers are used.
