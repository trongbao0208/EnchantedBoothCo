Good to know

Booking basics

Deposit

A 50% deposit secures your date. Your booking is not reserved until the deposit is received and confirmed.

Reschedules

Deposits are non-refundable but may be applied to a rescheduled date, subject to availability.

Setup

We arrive early to set up before your guests do. The booth needs a standard power outlet and Wi-Fi for instant sharing. Setup and breakdown time is not counted in your service hours.

Insured

Enchanted Booth Co. carries general liability insurance and can provide a certificate of insurance (COI) on request.
